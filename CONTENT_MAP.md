# CONTENT MAP

## 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This document transforms the approved V2 architecture into an editorial and content wireframe. It defines what each page must communicate, in what order, for whom, with what evidence, and toward which conversion.

It does not define visual design, UI components, final commercial copy, prices, claims, or implementation code.

### Content status vocabulary

- `VERIFIED`: supported by the approved architecture, brief, or an explicit structural requirement.
- `PROVISIONAL`: working direction that still needs editorial or business confirmation.
- `DATA NOT VERIFIED`: relevant information without an approved reliable source.
- `BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED`: the business must choose or validate the formulation before publication.

## 1.1 BUSINESS VALIDATION BASELINE

The validated commercial structure is:
- Educación Financiera: Colegios y Familias
- Consultoría de Gestión: Empresas

This baseline must guide Home and audience messaging. The old idea of a generic corporate financial education offer for employees is not valid under the current commercial model.

## 2. GLOBAL CONTENT PRINCIPLES

- Umbrella positioning: **Formación financiera diseñada para tu realidad.** `PROVISIONAL`
- The Home must route visitors to Colegios, Empresas, or Familias.
- Audience pages own their specific commercial intent; Contacto is a global fallback, not the only conversion path.
- Do not invent testimonials, metrics, clients, prices, years of operation, or outcomes.
- References such as `+200 familias`, `$10 por estudiante`, `$250 por un grupo de 5`, `hasta 20 personas`, and `4–6 semanas` remain unconfirmed.
- Contradictory sources must be preserved and marked `[BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED]`.
- H1 directions describe intended meaning only; they are not final copy.
