# SEO MAP

## 1. SEO OBJECTIVE

This SEO plan validates the proposed V2 information architecture against the real commercial language and search behavior that each audience is likely to use. It does not rely on unverified search-volume claims.

The focus is to make sure the site answers the right searches with the right page intent, without stuffing keywords or creating cannibalization between service pages and resources.

## 5. POTENTIAL CANNIBALIZATION AND OVERLAP

### Shared terms across pages
The following terms are likely to overlap and must be managed deliberately:
- educación financiera
- finanzas para estudiantes
- finanzas para familias
- finanzas para empresas
- programas de finanzas

### Recommended ownership model
- Home: broad brand and category discovery
- Service pages: exact audience-specific commercial terms
- Resources: educational, explanatory, and question-based queries

### Practical protection against cannibalization
- Avoid duplicating the exact same commercial title and intro across multiple pages.
- Make each service page own a different audience problem and user need.
- Use internal links from Resources to service pages, and from service pages to relevant Resource content that supports the offer.
- Keep the homepage broad but not duplicative of the landing pages.

## 6. URL DECISION: OPTION A VS OPTION B

### Option A
- /colegios
- /empresas
- /familias

### Option B
- /educacion-financiera-colegios
- /formacion-financiera-empresas
- /educacion-financiera-familias

### UX
Option A advantages:
- cleaner and more memorable
- easier to navigate
- aligns with a polished brand experience

Option A disadvantages:
- less explicit to search engines and users about the topic at first glance

Option B advantages:
- more descriptive immediately
- clearer to users and crawlers
- stronger keyword signal in the URL

Option B disadvantages:
- longer and less elegant
- less brand-friendly
- harder to read in menus and navigation

### SEO
Option A advantages:
- strong if the page title, H1, meta description, and internal links provide the necessary topical relevance
- easier to maintain a clean keyword architecture without enforcing exact-match URL strings

Option A disadvantages:
- less explicit keyword-rich context in the URL itself

Option B advantages:
- strong topical clarity
- stronger literal search alignment for some key phrases

Option B disadvantages:
- can feel artificial if overused, and may create an overly keyword-stuffed appearance in URLs

### Scalability
Option A advantages:
- shorter and easier to extend across the site
- cleaner as a brand architecture

Option A disadvantages:
- may become ambiguous in larger content ecosystems if more audience pages are added

Option B advantages:
- easier to scale in a content-heavy SEO environment
- clearer if multiple service pages need exact separation

Option B disadvantages:
- URL length increases and can become cumbersome across the site

### Readability
Option A is simpler and more elegant.
Option B is more descriptive but longer.

### Maintainability
Option A is easier to maintain in general, especially when building a site with a clear commercial architecture.
Option B is maintainable but more verbose and requires discipline in content and internal-link patterns.

### Brand architecture
Option A supports a cleaner, more premium commercial brand experience.
Option B supports a more topic-first, SEO-explicit approach.

### Recommendation
The better default for a clean commercial site is Option A for the public-facing IA and navigation, while still making sure the title, headings, metadata, and internal links carry the specific commercial intent. If the business later decides it needs stronger keyword clarity in URLs, descriptive slugs can be layered in as a secondary strategy without abandoning the cleaner brand structure.

This recommendation is not based on search volume alone; it balances UX, SEO, scalability, and brand consistency.
